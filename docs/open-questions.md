## Business
1. ~~Season calendar~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): signup is open all year. Services have start/end dates and hand off to variants that ask different things (student storage ↔ off-season student storage) at a set date and time; the form is assembled from the selected services, so non-student services (IKEA) don't collect student/school info. Designed in `domain-model.md#service`. Dates, services, and fields are owner configuration (see Q2). Reports run on any time frame (Josh, 2026-10-14), so year-round work needs no special season attribution.
2. ~~How is storage priced / where does the price list live?~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): beyond the marketing site, it's the owner's discretion. Design: owner-maintained rate card in admin (AI identifies, table prices), and review overrides grow the table. A public price page is out of scope. Services and their fields are likewise owner-configured from typed field building blocks rather than specified by us (`domain-model.md`).
3. ~~Who types invoice lines / when is storage billed?~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): the owner types them from written crew sheets ("contracts") and invoices main storage shortly after pickups, so customers pay before delivery; items aren't released unpaid; declines are chased manually today. Design: digital contract on company Android tablets (paperless), automated decline emails + retries, then a Collections list for the owner; delivery on hold while past due. Billing schedule = invoice approval: approved invoices charge immediately, then automated failure follow-up (Josh, 2026-10-14).
4. ~~Starting prices~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): the inferred prices are a fine start; the full seed list mined from 2025–26 invoice text is in `baseline-metrics.md#prices-observed-in-invoices`. The owner edits everything in admin.
5. ~~Confirmation number reuse; abroad storage~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): reuse is deliberate because the number labels items in the warehouse and short numbers are easier. Abroad items usually stay into the next year, sometimes longer, and are set aside in the warehouse. Decision: keep the confirmation number as the single shared number (splitting it is a hard sell and risks wrong-item handoffs), keep lowest-free reuse, also reserve numbers still on the shelf from earlier seasons. Customer-printed labels carry number, school color, and branding (`domain-model.md#booking-was-contract`). No prefixes; 4–5 digits is a good problem to have. Abroad bookings are billed whenever the owner approves their invoices (Josh, 2026-10-14).
6. ~~Crews, employees, trucks, warehouses~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): unknown and variable, so all of it is configuration (as in legacy). Employees have roles, crews are formed day by day, and jobs are assigned to crews per day (`domain-model.md#appointment-job-dispatch`). He'll buy whatever tablets and cost-effective data plans (Mint, Ting, etc.) the tracking needs, once he sees it working.
7. ~~Biggest pain~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): customer communication in peak season (mostly where/when and questions answerable from the site or emails), then staffing (college students; streamlined crews and performance-based crew pay may help), then manual data entry and billing. Designed in `features/customer-communication.md`. No volume baseline needed: any automation is a big change for him. Performance pay is his call, supported by reports for any time frame.
8. ~~Claims~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): handled manually by the owner. Design: online claim submission into a queue he works periodically, evidence from pickup photos plus required delivery photos, resolution through the ledger. Claim window: 7 days after delivery. Tips: a box per crew member, pre-filled with a default, editable; each gets the full amount (CSS absorbs the card fee) (Josh, 2026-10-14).
9. ~~Non-summer products~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): all business runs through the system. The owner often adds services to existing bookings without a new signup; the data agrees (about 45% of priced lines reference a service not on the booking). Design: a structured Add service flow, and every line belongs to a service on the booking (`domain-model.md#booking-was-contract`).
10. ~~Rails-hosted pages~~ Answered (Josh, 2026-10-14): unused; WordPress is the only public site. Not migrated. Signup reference data (schools, school events, school services, dorms) is still used and is migrated.
11. Accounting and payroll systems (QuickBooks? Gusto?) — what exports do they need? **Tabled** (Josh, 2026-10-14): not part of the pitch. v1 offers generic CSV exports (payments, hours, tips); specific integrations come later once we know his tools.
